# Executive Playbook: Data Sanity Checks, Triangulation & Quantitative Modelling

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **sanity checks and quantitative modelling** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
Before modelling, size the answer with Fermi estimation, check for outliers, and triangulate bottom-up against top-down figures. Business cases then convert assumptions into cash flows, NPV, IRR and payback.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Build a bottom-up Fermi estimate by multiplying independent factors.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Build a top-down estimate from a different source and compare.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Scan the data series for outliers using the IQR rule.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Model cash flows and compute NPV, IRR and payback.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Flag correlation-causation risks before presenting.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Precision theatre on weak assumptions
- Reading correlation as causation

## Worked example
**Sizing an expense-software market and evaluating the build.** Bottom-up sizing gave 2.16B and top-down 2.9B, within a factor of 1.4, enough to proceed. A monthly revenue series contained an outlier (260 vs. a 120 range) traced to a one-off contract; removing it changed the trend. The investment showed a positive NPV at 12 percent.

## Quality bar
- I triangulated two independent estimates
- I examined outliers before averaging
- My model shows NPV, IRR and payback
- I listed my top three assumptions
