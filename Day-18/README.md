# Day 18 (2026-08-18): Persuasion Without Authority & Executive Presence

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 6 - Influencing & Stakeholder Alignment

## Framework breakdown
Influence without authority relies on pre-wiring decisions through one-to-one conversations, aligning incentives, and building a coalition before the meeting. A 60-second pitch states the problem, the ask and the evidence.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Map decision-makers and likely stance.
2. Hold one-to-one pre-wire conversations starting with blockers.
3. Tailor the pitch to each stakeholder's incentives.
4. Compute coalition support weighted by power.
5. Use the meeting to confirm the decision, not to debate it.

## Real-world case study: Approving an AI forecasting pilot budget
An analytics lead needed approval from eight people. After pre-wiring the CFO and Plant Operations Head, the coalition index moved from near zero to clearly positive. The meeting lasted 20 minutes and approved the pilot.

## Common pitfalls
- Surprising executives in the meeting
- Pitching features instead of incentives
