# Executive Playbook: Persuasion Without Authority & Executive Presence

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **persuasion and pre-wiring** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
Influence without authority relies on pre-wiring decisions through one-to-one conversations, aligning incentives, and building a coalition before the meeting. A 60-second pitch states the problem, the ask and the evidence.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Map decision-makers and likely stance.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Hold one-to-one pre-wire conversations starting with blockers.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Tailor the pitch to each stakeholder's incentives.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Compute coalition support weighted by power.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Use the meeting to confirm the decision, not to debate it.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Surprising executives in the meeting
- Pitching features instead of incentives

## Worked example
**Approving an AI forecasting pilot budget.** An analytics lead needed approval from eight people. After pre-wiring the CFO and Plant Operations Head, the coalition index moved from near zero to clearly positive. The meeting lasted 20 minutes and approved the pilot.

## Quality bar
- I know each decision-maker's incentive
- I pre-wired blockers first
- My 60-second pitch states the ask
- I computed coalition support
