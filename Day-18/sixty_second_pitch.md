# 60-Second Pitch Template

**Problem (15s):** "Stock-outs cost us 12M a year and forecasting errors are the main cause."
**Ask (10s):** "I am asking for a 6M, six-month pilot in two plants."
**Evidence (20s):** "A prior pilot cut errors 28 percent; the payback is 14 months; governance sits with the Chief Risk Officer."
**Incentive for you (10s):** "For you, this means fewer expedited shipments and a measurable KPI for the quarter."
**Next step (5s):** "Can I walk you through the numbers on Thursday?"

## Pre-wire sequence
1. Chief Financial Officer (blocker, high power): payback evidence.
2. Plant Operations Head (blocker): rollout workload plan.
3. Chief Data Officer (supporter): ask them to co-present data readiness.
4. Steering committee: confirm, do not debate.
