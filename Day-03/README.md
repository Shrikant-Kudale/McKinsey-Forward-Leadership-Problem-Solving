# Day 03 (2026-08-03): Building Deliberate Learning Loops & Habit Architecture

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 1 - Foundations of Adaptability & Growth Mindset

## Framework breakdown
The 70-20-10 model suggests roughly 70 percent of development comes from experience, 20 percent from exposure to others and 10 percent from formal education. Learning compounds when each experiment closes a loop of act, get feedback, reflect and adjust, and when habits are anchored to cues and tracked for consistency rather than intensity.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Set a weekly learning budget and split it across 70-20-10.
2. Choose one experiment per week with a hypothesis about what will improve.
3. Schedule feedback from a named person within 48 hours of the experiment.
4. Reflect for 15 minutes: what happened, why, what changes next.
5. Track consistency and trend, not just total hours.

## Real-world case study: Junior ML engineer builds a 6-week learning loop
An associate engineer wanted to become independent on model deployment. Instead of a course binge she shipped one small deployment per week, asked a senior reviewer for feedback within two days, and logged reflections. Consistency above 70 percent and a rising trend predicted her promotion to lead a deployment within the quarter.

## Common pitfalls
- Measuring hours consumed instead of experiments completed
- Skipping the reflection step when busy
