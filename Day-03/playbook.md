# Executive Playbook: Building Deliberate Learning Loops & Habit Architecture

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **learning loops and habits** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
The 70-20-10 model suggests roughly 70 percent of development comes from experience, 20 percent from exposure to others and 10 percent from formal education. Learning compounds when each experiment closes a loop of act, get feedback, reflect and adjust, and when habits are anchored to cues and tracked for consistency rather than intensity.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Set a weekly learning budget and split it across 70-20-10.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Choose one experiment per week with a hypothesis about what will improve.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Schedule feedback from a named person within 48 hours of the experiment.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Reflect for 15 minutes: what happened, why, what changes next.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Track consistency and trend, not just total hours.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Measuring hours consumed instead of experiments completed
- Skipping the reflection step when busy

## Worked example
**Junior ML engineer builds a 6-week learning loop.** An associate engineer wanted to become independent on model deployment. Instead of a course binge she shipped one small deployment per week, asked a senior reviewer for feedback within two days, and logged reflections. Consistency above 70 percent and a rising trend predicted her promotion to lead a deployment within the quarter.

## Quality bar
- My weekly hours roughly follow a 70-20-10 split
- I run one experiment per week
- I receive feedback within 48 hours
- I review the tracker every Sunday
