# Executive Playbook: Self-Awareness & Energy Management

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **self-awareness and energy management** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
Sustainable performance depends on managing energy, not just time: physical, emotional, mental and purpose. Renewal cycles (work in focused sprints, recover deliberately) and early-warning indicators such as declining sleep and rising stress prevent burnout.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Log sleep, deep work, movement, breaks and stress for two weeks.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Compare each metric with your target and compute consistency.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Identify two metrics trending the wrong way.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Design renewal rituals for each energy type.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Set a trigger: if two warnings persist for a week, reduce load.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Treating rest as a reward instead of an input
- Ignoring trends because averages look fine

## Worked example
**Consultant managing cognitive load across two engagements.** A consultant logged metrics for 14 days. Sleep consistency was 43 percent and stress exceeded target on half the days, flagged as early warnings. Protecting two deep-work blocks and a fixed shutdown time restored sleep consistency to 70 percent within a month.

## Quality bar
- I track at least four energy metrics
- I know my early-warning signals
- I have renewal rituals for each energy type
- I set a load-reduction trigger
