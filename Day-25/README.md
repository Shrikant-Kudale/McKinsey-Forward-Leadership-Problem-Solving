# Day 25 (2026-08-25): Self-Awareness & Energy Management

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 9 - Emotional Intelligence & Sustainable Resilience

## Framework breakdown
Sustainable performance depends on managing energy, not just time: physical, emotional, mental and purpose. Renewal cycles (work in focused sprints, recover deliberately) and early-warning indicators such as declining sleep and rising stress prevent burnout.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Log sleep, deep work, movement, breaks and stress for two weeks.
2. Compare each metric with your target and compute consistency.
3. Identify two metrics trending the wrong way.
4. Design renewal rituals for each energy type.
5. Set a trigger: if two warnings persist for a week, reduce load.

## Real-world case study: Consultant managing cognitive load across two engagements
A consultant logged metrics for 14 days. Sleep consistency was 43 percent and stress exceeded target on half the days, flagged as early warnings. Protecting two deep-work blocks and a fixed shutdown time restored sleep consistency to 70 percent within a month.

## Common pitfalls
- Treating rest as a reward instead of an input
- Ignoring trends because averages look fine
