# Day 16 (2026-08-16): Stakeholder Mapping & Power-Interest Grids

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 6 - Influencing & Stakeholder Alignment

## Framework breakdown
Stakeholders differ in power (ability to affect the outcome) and interest (how much the outcome affects them). The power-interest grid, combined with stance from champion to blocker, gives each stakeholder a tailored engagement approach.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. List every stakeholder and the decision they influence.
2. Score power and interest from 1 to 10.
3. Score stance from -2 (blocker) to +2 (champion).
4. Assign an engagement strategy by quadrant.
5. Plan the first conversation with each high-power blocker.

## Real-world case study: Data-platform migration across engineering and business teams
A migration affected nine groups. Mapping showed the Security Lead had high power and a mild blocking stance because of access-control concerns; an early design review addressed them. Sales Ops, with low power but high interest, were kept informed and became vocal supporters.

## Common pitfalls
- Ignoring quiet high-power stakeholders
- Mapping once and never updating
