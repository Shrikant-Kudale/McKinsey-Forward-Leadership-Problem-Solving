# Day 20 (2026-08-20): Inclusive Collaboration & Productive Conflict Resolution

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 7 - High-Performing Teams & Collaboration

## Framework breakdown
Cognitive diversity improves problem solving when task conflict is encouraged and relationship conflict is contained. Structured protocols (silent brainstorming, round-robin, consent decisions) ensure quieter voices are heard.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Separate task conflict (ideas) from relationship conflict (people).
2. Use silent writing before discussion.
3. Run a round-robin so every voice is heard once.
4. Decide with a consent or consensus protocol.
5. Capture the decision and dissent for later review.

## Real-world case study: Product and engineering disagree on roadmap scope
Two leaders argued for weeks. A structured session with silent idea writing and a consent protocol surfaced a third option neither had proposed, and the decision log recorded the dissent so it could be revisited after the first release.

## Common pitfalls
- Letting the loudest voice decide
- Avoiding conflict until it becomes personal
