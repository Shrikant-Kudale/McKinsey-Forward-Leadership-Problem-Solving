# Executive Playbook: Inclusive Collaboration & Productive Conflict Resolution

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **inclusive collaboration and conflict** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
Cognitive diversity improves problem solving when task conflict is encouraged and relationship conflict is contained. Structured protocols (silent brainstorming, round-robin, consent decisions) ensure quieter voices are heard.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Separate task conflict (ideas) from relationship conflict (people).

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Use silent writing before discussion.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Run a round-robin so every voice is heard once.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Decide with a consent or consensus protocol.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Capture the decision and dissent for later review.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Letting the loudest voice decide
- Avoiding conflict until it becomes personal

## Worked example
**Product and engineering disagree on roadmap scope.** Two leaders argued for weeks. A structured session with silent idea writing and a consent protocol surfaced a third option neither had proposed, and the decision log recorded the dissent so it could be revisited after the first release.

## Quality bar
- I separated task and relationship conflict
- Quieter voices were heard
- We used a decision protocol
- Dissent is recorded
