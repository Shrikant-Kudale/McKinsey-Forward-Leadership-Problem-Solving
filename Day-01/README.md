# Day 01 (2026-08-01): Navigating the Future of Work: VUCA/BANI & Capability Baseline

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 1 - Foundations of Adaptability & Growth Mindset

## Framework breakdown
VUCA (volatility, uncertainty, complexity, ambiguity) describes environments that are hard to read; BANI (brittle, anxious, nonlinear, incomprehensible) describes ones where familiar playbooks fail abruptly. Adaptable professionals pair a growth mindset, the belief that capability is built through deliberate effort, with a measured baseline so progress is visible rather than assumed.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Map your current work environment against the four VUCA and four BANI dimensions.
2. Audit fixed vs. growth self-talk across your last five setbacks.
3. Score yourself on eight capability dimensions to create a baseline.
4. Choose two capabilities to stretch over the next 30 days.
5. Define one observable weekly practice for each stretch capability.

## Real-world case study: Fintech payments team hit by a mid-roadmap regulatory change
A regulator changed settlement-reporting rules six weeks before a planned launch. The team that had mapped its environment as BANI re-sequenced the roadmap in two days, while a team anchored in fixed-mindset language (we are not a compliance team) lost a sprint to blame. The difference was an explicit capability baseline and a named stretch goal.

## Common pitfalls
- Treating mindset as personality instead of a trainable habit
- Scoring yourself generously with no evidence
