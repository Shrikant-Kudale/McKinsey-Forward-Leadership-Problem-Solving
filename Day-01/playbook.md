# Executive Playbook: Navigating the Future of Work: VUCA/BANI & Capability Baseline

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **VUCA/BANI and mindset baseline** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
VUCA (volatility, uncertainty, complexity, ambiguity) describes environments that are hard to read; BANI (brittle, anxious, nonlinear, incomprehensible) describes ones where familiar playbooks fail abruptly. Adaptable professionals pair a growth mindset, the belief that capability is built through deliberate effort, with a measured baseline so progress is visible rather than assumed.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Map your current work environment against the four VUCA and four BANI dimensions.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Audit fixed vs. growth self-talk across your last five setbacks.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Score yourself on eight capability dimensions to create a baseline.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Choose two capabilities to stretch over the next 30 days.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Define one observable weekly practice for each stretch capability.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Treating mindset as personality instead of a trainable habit
- Scoring yourself generously with no evidence

## Worked example
**Fintech payments team hit by a mid-roadmap regulatory change.** A regulator changed settlement-reporting rules six weeks before a planned launch. The team that had mapped its environment as BANI re-sequenced the roadmap in two days, while a team anchored in fixed-mindset language (we are not a compliance team) lost a sprint to blame. The difference was an explicit capability baseline and a named stretch goal.

## Quality bar
- I can describe my work environment using VUCA and BANI vocabulary
- I have identified at least three fixed-mindset phrases I use
- I scored my eight capability dimensions with evidence
- I committed to two stretch capabilities with weekly practices
