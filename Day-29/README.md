# Day 29 (2026-08-29): Full Business Case Simulation: Retail Margin & Digital Turnaround

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 10 - Synthesis, Action Planning & Career Architecture

## Framework breakdown
The capstone case walks the full consulting arc: SCQ framing, MECE issue tree, hypothesis-driven analysis, financial model and a Pyramid Principle board memo. A regional retailer must lift thin margins while digital channels grow.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Frame the problem with SCQ and a SMART statement.
2. Build a MECE profit tree by channel and cost type.
3. Run the driver simulator to rank levers.
4. Pressure-test the numbers with sanity checks.
5. Write the board memo with the answer first.

## Real-world case study: Regional retailer: 85.8M revenue, 1.3 percent operating margin
In-store, online and click-and-collect channels produce 18.3M contribution against 17.2M fixed costs, leaving 1.1M profit. Online contributes the lowest margin per transaction because fulfilment cost is high. Cost levers dominate: a 5 percent variable-cost programme is worth about 3.4M versus 0.4M from mix shift.

## Common pitfalls
- Recommending a digital build-out without checking channel margins
- Presenting levers without quantifying them
