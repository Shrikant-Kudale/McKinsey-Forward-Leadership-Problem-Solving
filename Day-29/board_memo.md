# Board Memo: Retail Margin and Digital Turnaround

**To:** Board of Directors | **From:** Engagement Team | **Date:** 2026-08-29

## Recommendation (answer first)
Approve a 12-month margin programme that targets about 5.7M of annual profit improvement, led by variable-cost reduction, price and promotion discipline, and fixed-cost control, while pausing further digital capacity spend until online fulfilment economics improve.

## Why: three supports
1. **Cost levers dominate.** On 85.8M revenue the business earns 1.1M profit (1.3 percent). A 5 percent reduction in unit variable cost is worth about 3.4M; a 2 percent price and promotion improvement about 1.7M; a 5 percent fixed-cost reduction about 0.9M.
2. **Online grows but earns least.** Online contribution is 12 per transaction against 14 in-store and 18 for click-and-collect because fulfilment is heavy. Shifting 5 percent of volume to the best-margin channel adds about 0.4M, so the channel mix matters but is secondary.
3. **Risk is manageable.** The plan is staged by quarter with monthly tracking of margin per transaction; downside protection comes from reversible supplier and promotion decisions.

## Next steps
- Q1: renegotiate top 20 supplier contracts and review promotion calendar.
- Q2: redesign online fulfilment (dark stores vs. store picking) and expand click-and-collect.
- Q3: reassess digital investment against margin per transaction.
