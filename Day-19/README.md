# Day 19 (2026-08-19): Psychological Safety & Team Norms

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 7 - High-Performing Teams & Collaboration

## Framework breakdown
Google's Project Aristotle found psychological safety to be the strongest predictor of team effectiveness. Teams build it with working agreements, blameless post-mortems, and leaders who model fallibility and curiosity.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Baseline the team on a short psychological safety survey.
2. Draft working agreements for meetings, feedback and decisions.
3. Adopt a blameless post-mortem format.
4. Model vulnerability: share one mistake and what you learned.
5. Re-survey after six weeks and compare.

## Real-world case study: Engineering team recovers after a production incident
After an outage the team ran a blameless post-mortem that focused on system conditions. Reported willingness to raise concerns rose in the next survey and near-miss reports tripled, which prevented two later incidents.

## Common pitfalls
- Calling it blameless while still hunting for a culprit
- Norms written once and never revisited
