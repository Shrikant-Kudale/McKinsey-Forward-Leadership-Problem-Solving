# Executive Playbook: Active Listening, Empathy & Difficult Conversations

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **active listening and feedback** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
Level 1 listening is internal (thinking about what to say), Level 2 is focused on the speaker, and Level 3 is global, sensing tone and context. The SBI model (Situation, Behaviour, Impact) gives specific, non-judgemental feedback.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Prepare: decide the outcome you want and the facts you hold.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Open with Situation and Behaviour, then state Impact.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Listen at Level 2 and paraphrase before responding.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Ask a curious question about constraints.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Agree on next steps and a follow-up date.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Jumping to solutions while the other person is still explaining
- Feedback that mixes several incidents

## Worked example
**Pushing back on an unrealistic launch deadline.** A product manager asked an engineer to deliver in three weeks what needed eight. Using SBI and a trade-off question (which two of these five features matter most?), the engineer reached an agreed scope and a six-week date without conflict.

## Quality bar
- I paraphrased before responding
- My feedback separated behaviour from judgement
- I asked about constraints
- We agreed on follow-up
