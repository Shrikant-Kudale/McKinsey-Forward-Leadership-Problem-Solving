# Day 17 (2026-08-17): Active Listening, Empathy & Difficult Conversations

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 6 - Influencing & Stakeholder Alignment

## Framework breakdown
Level 1 listening is internal (thinking about what to say), Level 2 is focused on the speaker, and Level 3 is global, sensing tone and context. The SBI model (Situation, Behaviour, Impact) gives specific, non-judgemental feedback.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Prepare: decide the outcome you want and the facts you hold.
2. Open with Situation and Behaviour, then state Impact.
3. Listen at Level 2 and paraphrase before responding.
4. Ask a curious question about constraints.
5. Agree on next steps and a follow-up date.

## Real-world case study: Pushing back on an unrealistic launch deadline
A product manager asked an engineer to deliver in three weeks what needed eight. Using SBI and a trade-off question (which two of these five features matter most?), the engineer reached an agreed scope and a six-week date without conflict.

## Common pitfalls
- Jumping to solutions while the other person is still explaining
- Feedback that mixes several incidents
