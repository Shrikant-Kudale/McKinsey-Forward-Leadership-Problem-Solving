# Pushback Scripts for Unrealistic Deadlines

## Script 1: Trade-off question (SBI + options)
"In the planning meeting (Situation) the launch date was set at three weeks (Behaviour). With the scope as written, my estimate is eight weeks, and I am worried we will ship something unstable (Impact). Which two of these five features matter most to the customer? I can deliver those in three weeks and the rest in a second release."

## Script 2: Evidence first
"Here is the breakdown: design 1 week, build 3 weeks, test 2 weeks, buffer 1 week. Which of these would you like to compress, and what risk are you willing to accept?"

## Script 3: Escalation with respect
"I want to commit to a date I can keep. Could we spend 15 minutes with {sponsor} to agree on scope and risk so we are aligned?"

## Listening levels checklist
- Level 1: Am I rehearsing my reply? Pause.
- Level 2: Can I paraphrase their concern in one sentence?
- Level 3: What does their tone or the room dynamic tell me?
