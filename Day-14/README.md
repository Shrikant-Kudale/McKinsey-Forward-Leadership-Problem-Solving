# Day 14 (2026-08-14): Narrative Storyboarding & SCQA Executive Decks

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 5 - Communicating for Impact

## Framework breakdown
SCQA (Situation, Complication, Question, Answer) frames the narrative arc. Action titles (full-sentence takeaways) make a deck readable by its titles alone, and horizontal flow checks that titles read as a coherent story.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Write the SCQA for the whole deck.
2. Draft one action title per slide as a full sentence with a number or verb.
3. Read titles only, in order: does the story hold?
4. Assign each slide one chart or table that proves its title.
5. Cut slides whose titles do not advance the answer.

## Real-world case study: Digital turnaround deck for a regional retailer
A 40-slide deck was reduced to 12 by horizontal flow. Titles such as Online margin trails stores by 7 points because fulfilment costs 18 percent of basket made the story self-explanatory, and the leadership team skipped the live walkthrough.

## Common pitfalls
- Topic titles such as Market overview
- Slides that carry two messages
