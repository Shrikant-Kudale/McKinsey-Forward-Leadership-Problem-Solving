# Executive Playbook: Narrative Storyboarding & SCQA Executive Decks

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **SCQA storyboards** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
SCQA (Situation, Complication, Question, Answer) frames the narrative arc. Action titles (full-sentence takeaways) make a deck readable by its titles alone, and horizontal flow checks that titles read as a coherent story.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Write the SCQA for the whole deck.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Draft one action title per slide as a full sentence with a number or verb.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Read titles only, in order: does the story hold?

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Assign each slide one chart or table that proves its title.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Cut slides whose titles do not advance the answer.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Topic titles such as Market overview
- Slides that carry two messages

## Worked example
**Digital turnaround deck for a regional retailer.** A 40-slide deck was reduced to 12 by horizontal flow. Titles such as Online margin trails stores by 7 points because fulfilment costs 18 percent of basket made the story self-explanatory, and the leadership team skipped the live walkthrough.

## Quality bar
- The SCQA fits in four sentences
- Every title is a full-sentence takeaway
- Reading titles alone tells the story
- Each slide has exactly one proof element
