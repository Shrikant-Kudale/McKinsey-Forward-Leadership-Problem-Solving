# Executive Playbook: 90-Day Personal Leadership & Career Development Blueprint

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **90-day leadership blueprint** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
A 90-day blueprint converts learning into outcomes: OKRs set direction, a skill-gap roadmap sequences development, mentors and sponsors accelerate access, and a personal board of directors provides diverse perspectives.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Write two or three objectives with measurable key results.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
List current skills versus the skills your target role requires.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Sequence development in 30-day blocks.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Identify a mentor, a sponsor and two peers; draft outreach messages.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Review progress every two weeks.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Objectives with no measurable key results
- Asking a sponsor for advice when you need advocacy

## Worked example
**Early-career engineer plans a move into a product-leadership track.** The engineer set one objective (lead a cross-functional discovery) with three key results, mapped skill gaps in stakeholder management and metrics, and asked a director to sponsor a discovery project. Within 90 days she led one customer study and presented to the VP.

## Quality bar
- My OKRs have measurable key results
- My skill-gap roadmap is sequenced
- I drafted outreach to a mentor and sponsor
- I scheduled bi-weekly reviews
