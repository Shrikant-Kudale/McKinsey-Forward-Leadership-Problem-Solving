# Day 30 (2026-08-30): 90-Day Personal Leadership & Career Development Blueprint

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 10 - Synthesis, Action Planning & Career Architecture

## Framework breakdown
A 90-day blueprint converts learning into outcomes: OKRs set direction, a skill-gap roadmap sequences development, mentors and sponsors accelerate access, and a personal board of directors provides diverse perspectives.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. Write two or three objectives with measurable key results.
2. List current skills versus the skills your target role requires.
3. Sequence development in 30-day blocks.
4. Identify a mentor, a sponsor and two peers; draft outreach messages.
5. Review progress every two weeks.

## Real-world case study: Early-career engineer plans a move into a product-leadership track
The engineer set one objective (lead a cross-functional discovery) with three key results, mapped skill gaps in stakeholder management and metrics, and asked a director to sponsor a discovery project. Within 90 days she led one customer study and presented to the VP.

## Common pitfalls
- Objectives with no measurable key results
- Asking a sponsor for advice when you need advocacy
