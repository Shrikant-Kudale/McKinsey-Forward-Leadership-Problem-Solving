# Mentor, Sponsor and Peer Outreach Templates

## Mentor request
Subject: 20 minutes of advice on moving into product leadership
"Hi {name}, I admire how you moved from engineering to leading discovery. I am building a 90-day plan to develop stakeholder and metrics skills. Could I ask for 20 minutes to get your view on my plan? I will send a one-page summary beforehand."

## Sponsor ask (advocacy, not advice)
"Hi {name}, I have led two customer studies and want to lead a cross-functional discovery next quarter. Would you be willing to recommend me to {decision-maker} for this project?"

## 90-day OKR example
Objective: Lead a cross-functional customer discovery. KR1: 12 customer interviews by day 45. KR2: opportunity-solution tree reviewed by two senior leaders by day 60. KR3: present findings to the VP by day 90.
