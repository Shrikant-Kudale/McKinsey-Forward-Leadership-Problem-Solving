# Executive Playbook: End-to-End Capstone: Executive Playbook & Decision Toolkit

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **capstone executive playbook** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
The capstone consolidates thirty days into one playbook: a master template library, a decision-support suite that runs every tool, and a retrospective that records what worked, what to practise, and what to teach.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Review every template and choose the ones you will reuse.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Run the toolkit menu to execute each analytical tool.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Complete the retrospective: wins, gaps, next experiments.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Publish the repository with a clear README.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Plan your next 30-day sprint.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Treating the repo as a trophy instead of a working toolkit
- Skipping the retrospective

## Worked example
**Capstone toolkit run.** The toolkit launcher runs all analytical tools from one menu, producing a consolidated view of your decision-support kit. The retrospective turns outputs into next-sprint commitments.

## Quality bar
- I ran the toolkit end to end
- I selected my reusable templates
- I completed the retrospective
- I defined my next sprint
