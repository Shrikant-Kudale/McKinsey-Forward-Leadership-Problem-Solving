# Executive Playbook: Time Sovereignty, Deep Work & Ruthless Prioritization

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **time sovereignty and deep work** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
The Eisenhower matrix sorts work by urgency and importance. Deep work blocks protect Quadrant 2 (important, not urgent) from the shallow work that expands to fill a week. Saying no constructively preserves capacity for the work that matters.

## Procedure
### Step 1
Log a week of time by activity.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Tag each activity with its Eisenhower quadrant and work type.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
Compute deep-work hours and Quadrant 2 share.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Choose two Q3 or Q4 activities to delegate, batch or decline.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Block calendar time for deep work and use a no-with-alternative script.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Treating every request as urgent
- Saying no without offering an alternative

## Worked example
**Senior analyst with a fragmented week.** The weekly log showed 49 hours with only 13 deep hours and 15 hours of Quadrant 3 interruptions. Moving status meetings to asynchronous updates and batching email lifted deep work to 18 hours the next week.

## Quality bar
- I logged a full week
- I know my deep-work hours
- I identified two activities to cut
- My calendar protects deep-work blocks
