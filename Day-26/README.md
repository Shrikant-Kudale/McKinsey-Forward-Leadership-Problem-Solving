# Day 26 (2026-08-26): Emotional Regulation Under Pressure & Executive Composure

**McKinsey Forward alignment:** Session 9 - Emotional Intelligence & Sustainable Resilience

## Framework breakdown
Composure is a skill: map your triggers, then insert a pause between stimulus and response. In a crisis, leaders slow their pace, restate facts, and assign clear roles to keep the team clear-headed.

## Step-by-step methodology
1. List your top five triggers and the body signals that precede them.
2. Practise Stimulus-Pause-Response with a breath or a note-taking pause.
3. In escalations, restate the facts and the next decision out loud.
4. Assign roles: incident lead, communicator, scribe.
5. Debrief afterwards: what did I do well, what will I change?

## Real-world case study: Production crisis with an angry customer on the call
During a data-loss incident the engineering manager paused, restated the facts, and assigned an incident lead and communicator. The customer's tone shifted once they heard a clear plan with timestamps, and the team resolved the issue in two hours.

## Common pitfalls
- Suppressing emotion instead of regulating it
- Going silent when the team needs direction
