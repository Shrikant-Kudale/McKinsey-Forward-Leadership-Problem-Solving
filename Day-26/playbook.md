# Executive Playbook: Emotional Regulation Under Pressure & Executive Composure

## Purpose
Give a repeatable, 60-minute routine for **emotional regulation under pressure** that you can run on any project.

## Core idea
Composure is a skill: map your triggers, then insert a pause between stimulus and response. In a crisis, leaders slow their pace, restate facts, and assign clear roles to keep the team clear-headed.

## Procedure
### Step 1
List your top five triggers and the body signals that precede them.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 2
Practise Stimulus-Pause-Response with a breath or a note-taking pause.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 3
In escalations, restate the facts and the next decision out loud.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 4
Assign roles: incident lead, communicator, scribe.

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

### Step 5
Debrief afterwards: what did I do well, what will I change?

**Deliverable:** one written paragraph or table that proves this step is complete.

## Watch-outs
- Suppressing emotion instead of regulating it
- Going silent when the team needs direction

## Worked example
**Production crisis with an angry customer on the call.** During a data-loss incident the engineering manager paused, restated the facts, and assigned an incident lead and communicator. The customer's tone shifted once they heard a clear plan with timestamps, and the team resolved the issue in two hours.

## Quality bar
- I know my top triggers
- I have a pause practice
- I assign roles during escalations
- I debrief afterwards
